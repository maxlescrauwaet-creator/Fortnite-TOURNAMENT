ℹ️ About & Points System

Hosted by WIIPY: This exclusive tournament series brings together 16 small content creators for pure competition, intense matches, and epic YouTube content!

How scoring works:

  • Per Round: 1st place = 3 points | 2nd place = 2 points | 3rd place = 1 point (others get 0 points).
  • Overall Match / Map Finale: At the end of an entire match/map session, extra points are awarded based on final standings:
    • 🥇 1st place: 25 points
    • 🥈 2nd place: 20 points
    • 🥉 3rd place: 15 points
    • 🏅 4th place: 10 points
    • 🏅 5th place: 5 points
    • Others get 0 bonus points.
